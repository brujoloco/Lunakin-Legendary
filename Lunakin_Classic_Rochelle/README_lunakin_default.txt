lunakin - EQ Legends skin v2 (default-code-exact)
Lunakin Quarmified art & colors refit onto the EverQuest Legends client.

Load in game:  /loadskin lunakin

Changes vs the Legends default skin (v3):
  - 8 texture sheets repainted by Lunakin swapped in place (same names):
    window_pieces01-04.tga, wnd_bg_dark_rock.tga, wnd_bg_light_rock.tga,
    scrollbar_gutter.tga, wnd_bg_mid_rock.tga (=Lunakin dark rock bg,
    restyles the stone-inset windows: bank, merchant, containers, ...)
  - buff.tga + lunpoint.tga added (Lunakin buff frames / round pointer)
  - EQUI_Animations.xml: Lunakin texture rects/tints on the shared piece
    names (frames, title bars, buttons, scrollbars, sliders, gauges, chat
    chrome) AND on the stone chrome families (A_InsetFrame*/A_FakeInset*/
    A_TinyFrame* := Lunakin A_BorderFrame* values). Everything else is
    byte-identical to the Legends default. No renames, no new symbols,
    templates/windows/SIDL untouched.
  - Lunakin ships no cursor/spell/gem/dragitem/loadscreen art - those stay
    Legends default (same as Lunakin-on-Quarm behavior).
